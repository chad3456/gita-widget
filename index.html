    08 HOW THE GUIDANCE THINKS

    Proportional
    Navigation.

    A guided missile does not chase the target's current position — that loses every race. Instead it nulls the rotation of the line of sight.

    If the bearing to the target stops changing while the range shrinks, the two are on a collision course. The missile commands lateral acceleration proportional to how fast that line is still rotating:

    a = N · Vc · λ̇

    • N navigation constant (3–5)
    • Vc closing velocity
    • λ̇ line-of-sight rotation rate
    LOS rate → 0 = collision course
