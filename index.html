How this map is built

Every point on Earth is coloured by an estimate of the door-to-door travel time from your chosen city — the fastest of four modes:

  • ✈ Air — time to your city's main airport (researched per city) + a security/boarding buffer (≈2 h international, 1.4 h domestic) + the great-circle flight at ~820 km/h effective (incl. taxi, climb and descent) + a distance-based connection penalty + the drive from the arrival airport to the destination.
  • 🚄 Rail — ~210 km/h inside real high-speed-rail regions (Europe, East Asia), ~110 km/h on conventional lines elsewhere. Land routes only.
  • 🚗 Drive — ~85 km/h effective with a 1.25× road-detour factor. Land routes only.
  • 🚶 Walk — 4.8 km/h (it only ever wins next door).

Ground modes are blocked across open water using a land mask, so the sea is crossed by air. The map uses an azimuthal-equidistant projection centred on your city — the classic isochronic layout, where straight-line distance from the centre is true.

Real data

504 large international airports with real coordinates (OurAirports, via the airports dataset). World geometry from world-atlas (Natural Earth). Airport-access times and HSR regions are researched approximations.

Infrastructure layers

  • 🛢 Oil & gas pipelines — 20 major world systems (Druzhba, Nord Stream, Power of Siberia, Keystone, Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan, West–East, TANAP/TAP, TAPI…), routed from real waypoints after OpenStreetMap and the Global Energy Monitor infrastructure trackers.
  • 🛰 Submarine cables — indicative routes of major systems (MAREA, FASTER, SEA-ME-WE, 2Africa…) in the style of TeleGeography's Submarine Cable Map.
  • ⚓ Major ports — the world's busiest container ports by throughput (UNCTAD / Lloyd's List rankings), at real coordinates.
  • ☁ Weather — live current conditions for your city from the free, open Open-Meteo API (no key); falls back gracefully when offline.

This is a transparent model, not a routing engine — travel-time figures are realistic estimates and the infrastructure routes are researched approximations for exploration, not survey-grade GIS. Built with D3 (geo + contour). Data: Natural Earth, OurAirports, OpenStreetMap, Global Energy Monitor, TeleGeography, UNCTAD, Open-Meteo.
