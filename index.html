01 DOCTRINE

We design machines that protect people — engineered to observe, decide and act at the edge of human reaction time, without ever removing the human from command.

Human-on-the-loop

Contested by design

Built to function with no GPS, no network and no second chances. Degraded-mode is the default mode.

Open architecture

Modular payloads and a published autonomy API. No black boxes, no vendor lock, no surprises in the field.
